UO Memento Mobile 0.2.12 — Surface configuration checks

Log out, save and stop your session, then install UO-Memento-Mobile-0.2.12.apk over your working app. Keep app data. No runtime reinstall or client reimport is needed.

1. Localized updates
   Keep Native Surface, Send changed regions enabled, and your existing Turnip, 1280x720 and FPS settings. Let world loading settle. Spend about one minute stationary while moving the cursor and opening/moving/closing inventory and menus. Check for cursor trails, stale rectangles, flicker or incorrect colors. Open the gear view to compare transferred/redrawn percentages and copy/Surface timing.

2. Same walking route
   Walk the same route as the 0.2.11 test for several minutes, including animated areas. Note whether occasional stutters are reduced, unchanged or worse. Full redraws during broad animation are expected; lower transfer percentages alone do not prove higher game FPS. Confirm audio and controller input remain normal.

3. Surface recreation
   Return to the launcher and reopen the client. Switch away and return, then lock/unlock once. Check that the complete view returns with no trails or missing patches. Save & close session, then Play again and confirm correct startup and persistence.

4. Resolution change and comparison
   If you normally use 800x600 or 1024x768, check that resolution on a separate client launch. For a full-frame comparison, log out/save/stop, turn Send changed regions off, and relaunch with every other option fixed. Re-enable it afterward. This update does not change game/capture pacing or asset preparation.

5. Export support logs
   Export from Journal after the checks. In client-presentation.log, native_surface.buffer_reconfigurations should be zero during ordinary steady-state five-second windows, with setup needed again for a new Surface reader or game-resolution change. It should no longer match the frame count on every window. Small updates can reduce surface_redraw_fraction; Android may still expand redraw bounds. Include your subjective stutter result and whether it followed walking, cursor/menu movement or app resume.
