UO Memento Mobile 0.2.11 — device checks

Log out, save and stop your current session, then install UO-Memento-Mobile-0.2.11.apk over Recovery. Keep app data. The launcher name changes to UO Memento Mobile; no runtime reinstall or client reimport is needed.

1. Launch and visual checks
   Use Play Memento with your existing Native Surface, Turnip, 1280x720 and FPS settings. After initial loading, move the cursor over the world and gumps; open/move/close inventory and menus; walk across animated areas. Check for cursor trails, stale rectangles, missing patches, flicker or changed colors. Verify audio and controller input remain normal.

2. Surface recreation
   Return to the launcher and reopen the client. Switch away from the app and return; lock/unlock once. Confirm the complete view is drawn correctly. Save & close session, then Play again and confirm startup/persistence still work. If you normally use 800x600 or 1024x768, check that resolution on a separate client launch.

3. Compare changed-region delivery
   In Client → Client options, Send changed regions defaults on. Keep resolution, graphics, FPS and other options fixed. After loading settles, spend about one minute stationary, one minute moving the cursor/opening gumps, and one minute walking. Open the gear view to inspect transferred/redrawn percentages and capture/copy/Surface timing. Export support logs from Journal as the regions-on sample.
   Log out and save/stop. Turn Send changed regions off, restart the client, and repeat in the same place with the same activity. Export another support archive as the full-frame sample. You can then re-enable the option and relaunch.
   Reduced transferred bytes alone are not proof of higher game FPS. Capture still reads the full X11 image; Surface may expand redraw bounds. The two samples let us compare diff/transport/copy/Surface costs and actual play behavior.

4. Alternate pacing/display
   If your normal test was at 30 FPS, check 60 on a separate launch without changing other options. Confirm Standard display still works if you normally use it. Send changed regions affects only Native Surface.

If you see stale pixels or trails, export support logs and note whether it followed cursor movement, gump movement, a resolution change or app resume. Turn Send changed regions off and restart for the full-frame path. Include regions-on/off samples and whether the display fell back to Standard display. Preserve your known-good save export before any unrelated restore test.
